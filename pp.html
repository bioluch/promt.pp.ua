5. Legal Basis for Processing (EU/EEA — GDPR)

Under the GDPR, We only process Your Personal Data when We have a valid legal basis:

  • Contract performance: processing necessary to provide the Service You requested (e.g. account management, running Your analysis).
  • Consent: for non-essential cookies, marketing communications, and any processing of Special Category Data — You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate interests: for fraud prevention, network and information security, and service improvement, balanced against Your rights and freedoms. You may object to processing based on this basis at any time (Section 12).
  • Legal obligation: processing necessary to comply with a legal requirement (e.g. tax and accounting records, responding to lawful authority requests).
  • Vital interests: in the rare case processing is necessary to protect someone's life.

8. International Data Transfers

We are based in Ukraine. Because Ukraine does not currently benefit from a European Commission adequacy decision, when You are located in the EU/EEA, UK, or Switzerland, We rely on the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission (and, where relevant, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum) as the legal mechanism authorizing the transfer of Your Personal Data to Us in Ukraine.

Our sub-processors are located outside Ukraine, and Your data may be transferred onward to them as follows:

  • Google LLC (United States) — used for AI/OCR processing and email services. We rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (DPF) where Google is self-certified for the relevant service, with Standard Contractual Clauses as a fallback, supported by a Transfer Impact Assessment.
  • Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd. (China) — used for AI processing. China does not benefit from an EU adequacy decision, so We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses together with a Transfer Impact Assessment evaluating Chinese law and practice, as the transfer mechanism for this provider.
  • Contabo GmbH (Germany) — our hosting provider is based within the EU/EEA, so no additional international transfer mechanism is required for hosting.

You may request a copy of the relevant safeguard (e.g. the SCCs We have executed with a given recipient) by contacting Us using the details in Section 17.

9. Data Retention

We keep Personal Data only as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Policy, applying the following defaults unless a longer period is required by law:

  • Account data: for as long as Your account is active, plus 10 days after closure to allow for reactivation and to comply with legal obligations.
  • Uploaded documents and test results: not stored after processing. Files You upload and the results generated from them are automatically deleted once Your result has been returned, unless You choose to save the result to Your account history.
  • Usage / analytics data: up to 1 month.
  • Billing records: not applicable — the Service does not currently process or store billing records, as no payment processor is in use.
  • Support correspondence: 1 year from the last interaction.
  • Backups: We do not maintain backup retention beyond the periods stated above.

Where We can no longer identify You, retained data is treated as anonymized and these limits no longer apply.
